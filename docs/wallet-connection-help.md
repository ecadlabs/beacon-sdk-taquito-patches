# Trouble connecting your wallet?

If the app says it couldn’t reach the WalletConnect relay, the app hasn’t been able to contact the service it uses to connect to your wallet.

1. Check that you can open a web page on the device running the app. If you’re scanning a QR code with a phone, check the phone’s internet connection too.
2. Open your wallet, return to the app, and choose **Connect wallet** again. If the app shows a new QR code, scan the new code.
3. If other sites load and pairing still fails, try another network you trust, such as mobile data. A firewall or network filter can block the relay even when ordinary websites load.

If you still can’t connect, contact the app’s support team. Include the error text, your wallet and browser names, and whether switching networks helped. Keep pairing codes, wallet recovery phrases, and private keys out of support messages.
